# Fork & Ship: 2-minute demo script

**0:00 Problem (15s)**
Every app founder hears "post on TikTok". They copy a video they liked, it gets 300 views, and they don't know why.
The video that worked was never about the topic. It was the structure: when the text lands, how long each shot is, what is said in the first second.

**0:15 Oriane read the winners (30s)** (dashboard, section 1)
We asked Oriane for every calorie-deficit and calorie-tracker TikTok since May and ranked them by views per follower.
A 27K-follower account with 641K views proves the format, not the fame.
Each card shows what Oriane extracted: green is what's said in the first 3 seconds, yellow ticks are text appearing on screen, plus sound, length and words per second.

**0:45 Fork (40s)** (section 3, original vs ours, play the video)
Anyone can say "fork a video". We do it from Oriane's per-frame data: text boxes blur the creator's caption out of each reference frame, timestamps guide the shots, and faces are never sent to the model.
85% of screen time is copied structure: same shots, framing, cut length, on-screen text. One hook line is tested. The last 15% is the app.
We copy structure, which no one owns, never footage or faces.

**1:25 Ship + learn (25s)** (section 4)
Outnumber's API uploads it and queues it on a TikTok account. Nothing is posted yet, it was built today. Next step: views come back through the same API and the best hook becomes the default.

**1:50 Why it sells Monday (10s)**
Outnumber already runs TikTok posting for paying app founders. This is the content step we were missing: one command, one new video per account per day.
